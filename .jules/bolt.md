## 2024-05-27 - [Performance: Avoid Math.sqrt in nested animation loops]
**Learning:** Found multiple instances where `distanceTo()` or manual `Math.sqrt()` were called inside $O(n^2)$ nested loops for node connection calculations in Canvas and Three.js scenes, which significantly degraded performance during high-frequency animation loops. Attempting to fix existing React hook dependencies in unrelated files during Bolt missions runs a high risk of breaking previously functioning logic due to codebase-specific rules preventing scope creep.
**Action:** Replace `distanceTo` and `Math.sqrt` with `distanceToSquared` and compare against squared thresholds in 3D/Canvas contexts. Ensure that Bolt optimizations strictly adhere to their focused scope without attempting to resolve pre-existing linting errors outside of the targeted feature.
