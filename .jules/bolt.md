## 2026-03-27 - Avoid Math.sqrt and distanceTo in High-Frequency Loops
**Learning:** Found multiple instances in ParticleBackground, FooterParticles, and Scene3D where `Math.sqrt` and Three.js's `distanceTo()` were used unconditionally inside nested loops (O(n²)) and `useFrame` animation loops. This is a critical performance bottleneck as calculating square roots is computationally expensive, especially when evaluating thousands of particle connections per frame.
**Action:** When calculating distances to apply thresholds in animation loops or particle systems, always optimize by computing the squared distance (`dx*dx + dy*dy` or `distanceToSquared()`) and comparing it against the squared threshold. Only calculate the actual square root if the elements fall within the threshold and the exact linear distance is required for subsequent math (like computing opacity).
